Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how IARE Motivation ("IARE", "the App", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the IARE Motivation mobile application.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

IARE Motivation is designed to collect only the information needed to provide the app experience.

We may collect or store the following information:

  • Profile information you provide, such as your name.
  • Mood and onboarding answers you choose to provide.
  • Selected quote topics and preferences.
  • Notification preferences, including notification count and preferred time range.
  • Favorite quotes, saved quotes, quote collections, recent quotes, and app customization settings.
  • Widget preferences, including selected widget topics, refresh frequency, and visual theme.
  • Purchase and subscription status, such as whether you have access to IARE Pro.

2. Information Stored On Your Device

Some information is stored locally on your device using Apple system storage, such as UserDefaults and Core Data. This may include your preferences, selected topics, favorites, collections, recent quotes, and widget settings.

If iCloud or Apple private database syncing is enabled by the App or your device, some app data may be synced through Apple services. Apple's handling of that data is governed by Apple's privacy policies and your Apple account settings.

3. Purchases and Subscriptions

IARE Motivation offers in-app purchases and subscriptions.

Payments are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive, collect, or store your credit card number, debit card number, bank information, or full Apple account payment details.

We may use RevenueCat, a third-party subscription management service, to help manage purchase status, subscriptions, entitlements, renewals, and restore purchases. RevenueCat may process information such as your App User ID, product identifiers, purchase status, transaction receipt information, app version, device information, and subscription entitlement status.

RevenueCat privacy policy:
https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

4. Notifications

If you allow notifications, IARE Motivation may schedule motivational notifications based on your selected preferences, such as notification count, time range, and selected topics.

You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings.

5. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services to provide app functionality, purchases, analytics, authentication, or platform integrations.

These services may include:

  • Apple App Store and StoreKit, for in-app purchases and subscription management.
  • RevenueCat, for subscription and entitlement management.
  • Apple iCloud or CloudKit, if app data syncing is used.
  • Facebook SDK, if enabled in the App, for platform integration or authentication features.

Each third-party service handles data according to its own privacy policy.

6. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Provide and personalize motivational quotes.
  • Save your preferences, topics, favorites, collections, and app settings.
  • Configure notifications and widgets.
  • Manage subscriptions, purchases, and premium access.
  • Restore purchases.
  • Maintain and improve the App.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Comply with legal, platform, and App Store requirements.

7. Tracking and Advertising

IARE Motivation does not sell your personal information.

IARE Motivation does not use your personal information for third-party advertising tracking unless this is clearly disclosed and, where required, permission is requested through Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework.

8. Data Sharing

We do not share your personal information with third parties except when necessary to:

  • Provide App functionality.
  • Process purchases and subscriptions.
  • Operate third-party services listed in this Privacy Policy.
  • Comply with legal obligations.
  • Protect the rights, safety, and security of users, the App, or others.

9. Data Retention

We keep app data for as long as needed to provide the App's features, maintain your preferences, manage purchases, or comply with legal obligations.

You may delete local app data by deleting the App from your device. Some data may remain in Apple iCloud, App Store purchase history, RevenueCat records, or backup systems according to the policies of those services.

10. Your Choices

You can:

  • Change your name, mood, topics, notifications, and other preferences inside the App.
  • Disable notifications in iOS Settings.
  • Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple ID subscription settings.
  • Restore purchases inside the App.
  • Delete the App from your device.
  • Contact us to request help with privacy-related questions.

11. Children's Privacy

IARE Motivation is not intended for children under the age required by applicable law to use digital services without parental consent.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

12. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information handled by the App. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

13. International Users

Your information may be processed in countries other than your country of residence, depending on the services used by Apple, RevenueCat, and other third-party providers. By using the App, you understand that information may be processed where those providers operate.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated Privacy Policy.
